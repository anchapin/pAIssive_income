# Team Review Etiquette & Collaboration Guidelines

## Code Review Etiquette

- Be respectful and constructive. Focus feedback on the code, not the person.
- Use clear, actionable comments. Explain why changes are needed.
- Celebrate good practices and improvements in reviews.
- Avoid nitpicking minor style unless it affects readability or consistency.
- Assume positive intent; clarify before criticizing.
- Respond to feedback promptly, or indicate when you need more time.
- Mark conversations as resolved only after addressing or discussing feedback.

## Communication

- Prefer public discussion in issues/PRs for transparency.
- Use threads for off-topic or tangent discussions.
- Ask for help early if blocked.
- Share context and decisions in documentation for future contributors.

> **Note:** See [Branching & Review Guidelines](../02_developer_guide/02_branching_and_review_guidelines.md#code-review-process) for the required review process.
